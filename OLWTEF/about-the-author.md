Johan Fourie is the Chair of Economics, History and Policy within the Department of Economics at Stellenbosch University. He is a founding member of the African Economic History Network, a former president of the Economic History Society of Southern Africa and director of LEAP, the Laboratory for the Economics of Africa's Past. He has published award-winning peer-reviewed articles and is a regular columnist for local newspapers. He runs the popular blog [ourlongwalk.com](https://www.ourlongwalk.com) (inspired by this book) as well as the *Our Long Walk* podcast, where he interviews leading social science scholars about the past, present and future of South Africa and Africa. He lives in Stellenbosch, South Africa.
